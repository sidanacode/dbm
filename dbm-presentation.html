09 / 09

The difference

With Alembic alone

  1. Developers create migrations independently.
  2. Git combines their branches.
  3. The team finds conflicts during merge or deployment.

With DBM and Alembic

  1. Developers share schema intent first.
  2. DBM finds cross-branch conflicts early.
  3. Alembic generates migrations after resolution.

DBM helps the team resolve the meaning of a change before Alembic records it.
